How it works

Three steps. A few minutes a day.

  1. 1

    Choose a topic

    Open Aimway and tap any subject. Set a daily goal of one to three topics, whatever fits your day.

  2. 2

    Take a 15-question quiz

    Answer 15 multiple-choice questions. Score 80% or more (12 of 15) to pass. Every retry brings a fresh set of questions.

  3. 3

    Review and move forward

    See what you got wrong with the right answers explained. Each pass moves you one step up your journey and earns 10 XP.

The Journey

A one-year climb, drawn as you learn

Your progress isn't a grade. It's a path up a mountain. Every passed topic adds a step, and the map grows upward toward a summit 389 steps away: one topic a day for a year, plus twelve monthly exams.

  • Monthly regions. Each calendar month is a new region of the map, ending in a checkpoint.
  • Combos. Pass three topics in a row on the same day for an extra step and +20 XP.
  • Friends on the map. See where your friends are on their own climb, right on your path.

Built for steady progress

Motivating, never noisy

Just enough to keep you coming back. No pressure, no shaming, no endless badges.

Learning streak

Pass at least one quiz a day to keep your streak. Today never breaks it, and your best streak is always kept.

  • 7 days +50 XP
  • 30 days +150 XP
  • 100 days +500 XP
  • 365 days +1000 XP

Monthly exam

Each month ends with a 20-question exam that mixes everything you studied. Pass it in its month for +2 steps and +100 XP, and unlock the next region. Results live in a calendar you can look back on.

Learn from every answer

Every quiz and exam ends with an answer review: which questions you missed, what you picked, the right answer and a short explanation.

Friends & leaderboard

Invite friends with your personal code and compare XP. Your leaderboard shows you and your direct friends only, never friends of friends.

Privacy you control

Friends see your name, avatar and XP. Never your email. Turn off "Show me to friends" any time and you disappear from their map and board.

Better questions, together

Spot a wrong answer, a typo or something out of date? Report any question in one tap and we'll check it.

FAQ

Questions, answered

When can I download Aimway?

Aimway is in development for iPhone (iOS 17 or later) and launches on the App Store soon. Android may follow later.

How long does a quiz take?

A topic quiz is 15 multiple-choice questions, usually a few minutes. You see your score and an answer review at the end, not during the quiz.

What happens if I fail a quiz?

Nothing bad. You just don't move forward yet. Read the answer review, then try again with a fresh set of questions. Free includes one retry a day; Pro has unlimited retries. A retry doesn't use up one of your three daily topics.

When does a day reset?

At midnight in your own time zone. Daily limits, combos and your streak all follow your local day.

What happens if I miss the monthly exam?

You can still pass it during the following month for +1 step and +50 XP. After that it closes. Your journey keeps going into the next region either way.

Does my progress follow me to a new phone?

Yes. Sign in with Apple, Google or email and your steps, XP and streak are tied to your account.

Is Aimway for kids?

Aimway is made for curious adults and older students who want to keep learning outside the classroom.
